What this does and does not prove

A verified chain shows that no saved file has been edited, deleted, or reordered since it was written. It cannot show that a chain was never shortened: deleting the newest record together with its last line in chain.log leaves a shorter chain that still verifies, because nothing inside the folder records how long the chain was supposed to be. Closing that gap needs the latest hash kept somewhere this computer does not control.
